## Why outreach follows original data, not the other way around
The first-party GSC data shows demand around football predictions, competition fixtures and historical match evidence. We should publish an accurately sourced, useful study before asking another publication to reference it. Good candidates include a limited, reproducible analysis of public NPFL fixture trends or a cup-rule explainer with documented sources. Avoid claims of guaranteed predictions, selective win-rate reporting and copying other publishers' content. Do not redistribute raw API-Football data or logos until the provider's licensing terms are checked.

## Vetting gates before any pitch
1. The target publication still operates and the editorial contact is verified on its own website.
2. The dataset is documented and independently reproducible; include methodology, timeframe, denominators, missingness and uncertainty.
3. Rights for any externally sourced football data permit publication of aggregate findings.
4. Outreach is individually relevant, respectful and not bulk unsolicited link spam. No payment for unlabelled editorial links.
5. Do not demand a dofollow link or exchange links; let the editor decide whether the research merits coverage.
6. Any later backlink is checked for live placement, contextual relevance and canonical URL before counting it in an SEO report.
