# Public copy

- Never mention AI, artificial intelligence, AI-assisted or AI-powered in website or app copy, including headings, descriptions, metadata, notifications, marketing content and generated match narratives.
- Use accurate neutral wording such as “match analysis”, “statistical analysis” or “data-based assessment”. Do not replace these terms with unsupported claims of human authorship, manual selection or personal review.
- Apply this rule to all new and edited public copy. Internal implementation identifiers and technical documentation need not be renamed.
